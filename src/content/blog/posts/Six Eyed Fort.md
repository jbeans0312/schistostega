At long last I have started catchup up on [TUMULUS](https://www.skeletoncodemachine.com/p/tumulus) projects! **Six Eyed Fort** is my creation for the third issue titled *Kick open the door*. My girlfriend and I have been playing a ton of dominoes recently, and it donned on me that the bones would probably work great as dungeon tiles: they're tactile, fit nicely together, common enough, and have just enough information (two panes with pips) to be useful.

Frankly I am not interested in writing a dev retrospective, maybe next time I will keep a journal as I go along. Seeing as I had a ton of fun working on Six Eyed Fort, and have a few more issues of TUMULUS to catch up on, I would count on it.
