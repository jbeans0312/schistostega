A 5 room wine 'n' dungeon I made as a demo for an ascii dungeon crawler my friend Nick and I are working on. The crawler itself is mostly an exercise to see if we enjoy making games together, but it's also a great excuse for me to make a bunch of fun dungeons.

This one is themed around **Büt the Unquenchable**, a demon that offers a beguiling wine to all who offer their cup. In this case, an expired "church" filled with thirsty skeletons.
# d4 Hooks
1. A wine collector has a thirst for a *devilishly* rare vintage buried in his grandfather's tomb.
2. An aspiring vintner will pay handsomely for an appraisal of the old vineyard on the hill.
3. A friend of yours never made it back from the tavern. Patrons swear he never left.
4. While enjoying the baron's party you spy a hooded figure sneaking down into the cellar. 
# d4 Possible Entry Points
1. Below a forgotten speakeasy down a wine-smelling alley.
2. Through a trapdoor hidden beneath a case of an unpopular libation.
3. Through a secret passage in the baron's wine cellar.
4. Through an impossible door in a barrel of wine.
# Environment
- Reeks of sour grapes
- Red-stained granite walls
- 10 ft vaulted ceilings

## [1] Entryway
- **Skeleton** slumped against a decorated iron **door**
- Red stained oak **barrels**
- Muffled squeaks & metallic rattling

**Skeleton**: White robes stained red. Holds an open key ring up to the lock. *In a drunken stupor he dropped his key into a barrel*.

**Door**: Heavy and iron. Locked. Demon (Büt) headed door knocker. Closed speakeasy door grill.

**Barrels**: Uncorked. Covered in scratches. 2 in 6 contain a swarm of hostile rats. 1 in 6 contains a key to the door. 3 in 6 contain red wine sediment.
****
**Swarm of Wine Soaked Rats**
**HP** 7 | **Morale** 6 | **Hard to hit** -d2 | **Claw/bite**: d2

THE FLOOR IS MOVING! These drunken rodents can occupy the same space as their target (attacks become d4).
## [2] Nave
- Cobwebbed wooden pews, some occupied by **skeletons**
- Stone **altar** with **horned statue**
- **Eastern door** closed and barred
- **Western door** closed and covered with red gunk

**Skeletons**: 6 total. Each holds a golden wine-stained goblet. Bones stained burgundy.
- Filling each goblet with wine opens the secret door to the left of the **altar**.
- Filling a skeleton's goblet with anything other than wine causes it to awake and attack.

**Altar**: Wide basalt slab adorned with a plaque reading "A toast to our good fortune".

**Statue**: Obsidian. Depicts 6 armed horned creature with unfurled tongues.

**Eastern door**: Wooden. Sealed with a metal bar.
- Note pinned with a corkscrew: "Our friend thinks himself above us now... We'll drink to that!"
- Groaning from the other side

**Western door**: Wooden. Sealed with a red gunk.
- Can be removed with simple tools or fire in d10 rounds (test Strength to expedite)
****
**BURGUNDY SKELETON**
**HP** 7 | **Morale** 8 | No armor | **Bony knuckles** d2 or **Goblet** d4 thrown
## [3] Tank
- Terrible rotting smell
- **Grey skinned creature** holds empty glass bottles
- Bones & bottles litter the floor
- **Drains** run either side of the room
- Small **trunk** tucked in the corner

**Drains**: Lead to the faucet in [4]

**Trunk**: Brown leather. Locked, *key is on the creature*.
- Fine clothes. A chipped **golden ring**.
- RING OF RETURNING
	- Chip off a piece of gold. Twice daily teleport to a chip. Destroyed after d20 chips.
****
**SPLATZED**
**HP** 15 | **Morale** N/A | **Covered in glass** -d2 + bleed | **Jagged fists** d4 + bleed | **Bleed** d2 damage for d4 rounds. Ignores armor.
## [4] Faucet Room
- A large **faucet** protrudes from the ceiling
- Floor is stained red
- Sticky footsteps lead north down a hallway. *A **lever** is just around the corner*

**Faucet**: Terracotta pipe. 2 in 6 chance to spawn a **Sediment** each round while activated.

**Lever**: Turns on the faucet when activated. Covered in a red gunk which can be removed with simple tools or fire in d10 rounds. While gunked, test Strength D14 to deactivate.
****
**SEDIMENT**
**HP** 8 | **Morale** 10 | No armor | **Inebriating tendril** d4 + special | **Special**: target cannot move until the Sediment is damaged.

THERE MUST BE SOME LEFT AT THE BOTTOM... The slime moves like jelly swirled in a glass. Pops into a puddle of wine fine enough for dead men. Its attacks have a 3 in 6 chance of filling a container held by the target with wine.
## [5] The World's Wine Cellar
- Wooden shelves lined with **bottles**
- Ornate mahogany **door** with golden hardware

**Bottles**: Of all varieties. From all ages, and locations. Marked with wax seals and gift tags from many people of note: royalty, tycoons, heroes. 

**The Octopus Door**: Impossibly ornate engraving (not done by a human hand). Golden octopus knocker & cuff port. 
- Locked. Leads to nothing if opened by normal means.
- Knocking on the door alerts the **octopus**.
	- Opens the door's cuff port if alerted. Extends a tentacle. 
	- Opens the door if given a bottle. The door leads to the cellar of the bottle's donor.
